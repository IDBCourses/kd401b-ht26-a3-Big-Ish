Ouroboros -[ʊərəˈbɒrəs]-

The Ouroboros (ʊərəˈbɒrəs) or Uroboros (jʊərəˈbɒrəs) is an ancient symbol often used to represent the eternal cycle of life which depicts a snake or dragon eating its own tail. The ouroboros is often interpreted as a symbol for eternal cyclic renewal or a cycle of life, death and rebirth. The creature's skin sloughing symbolises the transmigration of souls.
