Ouroboros -[ʊərəˈbɒrəs]-

" The elder wyrm that devours its own tail "

Ouroboros (οὐροβόρος, meaning “tail-devouring”)
Within its coil lie the passing of ages, the fading of flesh,
and the return of all things to their beginning.
As the serpent sheds its withered skin,
so the soul leaves its mortal vessel and passes beyond the veil.

Nothing that passes beyond the veil is wholly lost.
The dust of kings returns to the earth,
and the embers of fallen realms kindle fires yet unseen.
What the serpent consumes, it brings forth anew.
Every ending carries within it the seed of a new beginning.




What is the Ouroboros?

November 11, 2025 by Ben Dover


Introduction

Seek neither beginning nor end within the sacred coil, for each leads to the other.
Though the stars grow dim and the names of gods fall silent, the great wyrm endures.
It consumes the age that has passed, and from that feast an age yet unborn shall rise.
